## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how I collect, use, and protect your information when you visit my website. I am committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about my data practices.

## Information I Collect

### Contact Form Information
The only personal information I collect is:
- **Email Address**: When you submit a message through my contact form
- **Message Content**: The message you choose to send me

### Information I Do NOT Collect
I do not collect, store, or track:
- Cookies or tracking pixels
- IP addresses
- Browsing behavior or analytics data
- Location information
- Device information
- Any other personal data beyond what you voluntarily provide through the contact form

## How I Use Your Information

The information you provide through the contact form is used solely to:
- Respond to your inquiry or message
- Communicate with you about potential opportunities or collaborations

I will never:
- Sell, rent, or share your information with third parties
- Use your email address for marketing purposes without your explicit consent
- Store your information longer than necessary to respond to your inquiry

## Data Storage and Security

- Contact form submissions are processed securely
- Your email address and message are stored only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry
- I implement reasonable security measures to protect your information
- No third-party analytics or tracking services are used on this website

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request information about what data I have collected about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent for me to contact you at any time

## Third-Party Services

This website does not integrate with any third-party services that would collect your data. The contact form is processed directly without involving external platforms.

## Updates to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## Contact Me

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how I handle your information, please contact me through the contact form on this website.
