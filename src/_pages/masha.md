Masha has an automatic water fountain, food dispenser, and litterbox! These should make caretaking pretty easy. I've outlined the main tasks and any supplies you might need below. I've also provided more info about Masha further down in this guide.

# 📋 Tasks

## Supplies
- **Extra food and water bowls:** Cabinet above the microwave
- **Backup litterbox and scoop:** Closet next to the bathroom
- **Food and litter refills:** Bins next to the litter robot
- **Litter robot trash bags:** Next to the litter robot
- **Spare dishrags:** Under the kitchen sink (for spilled water)
- **Cleaning supplies for messes:** Yellow multisurface antibacterial spray under kitchen sink + paper towels
- **Treats and extra toys:** wicker cupboard with the orb light on top, second shelf from top
- **Cat carrier backpack:** Closet next to the bathroom

## Daily Tasks

### ☑️ Food Dispenser
  
  Should dispense food at 5:30 AM, 12:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 11:00 PM.
  
  - **If you're around at one of these times and dispenser doesn't run:** let me know that the dispenser is broken and fill a spare bowl of food for her
  - **If her food bowl is empty (she usually has at least some food left over from previous feedings)**: fill a spare bowl of food for her

### ☑️ Water Fountain

  - **If fountain isn't running:** Check if the basin is empty; if it is, fill water to 1 inch below max fill line. If it already has water, put out a spare bowl of water for her and let me know that the fountain broke.
  - **If fountain is overflowing:** Unplug it, clean up spilled water, dump some water out from the basin (this only happens if it's been overfilled)

### ☑️ Litter Robot

  - **If litter robot has more than 2-3 visible poops (ie hasn't been running automatically):** Press the **cycle** button
  - **If cycle button doesn't work:** Let me know that the litter robot isn't cycling, scoop it with the blue scoop

### ☑️ Playtime

  - See About Masha section for favorite toys, activities, etc!

## Weekly Tasks

*Weekly maintenance tasks for the auto-feeder, water fountain, and litter box will be completed before I leave, so no action needed unless I am gone for more than one week. I will let you know if this is the case.*

### ☑️ Water Fountain
1. Unplug the fountain
2. Put out spare water bowl while cleaning
3. Disassemble and clean basin - with soap and sponge in kitchen sink, filter - with water, located in plastic compartment. Don't clean the pump.
4. Refill about 1 inch below max fill line to prevent overflow
5. Reassemble and plug back in

### ☑️ Food Dispenser
1. Save remaining food in a spare bowl
2. Clean food bowl with soap and sponge
3. Dry bowl (dishrag or air dry)
4. Put bowl back and return saved food

### ☑️ Litter Robot

**Empty waste drawer:**

1. Open drawer at bottom
2. Remove full bag and put in trash
3. Replace with empty bag (extras next to litterbox)
4. Press **reset** button

**Top off litter:**

1. Check litter level against max fill line
2. Add scoops if below the line

# 😻 About Masha

## House Rules & Safety
- ❌ **Keep her off the porch**
- ❌ **Ideally keep her out of closets and cabinets.** Exception: she can access the bedroom closet, this is fine (door doesn't latch)
- ✅ **Open windows on nice days** for her enjoyment, but **always close before leaving**

## Handling & Petting
- ✅ **Loves:** Chin scratches and regular petting
- ❌ **Doesn't like:** Belly rubs
- **Picking up:** She's fine with it but will usually wriggle out after a few minutes

## Health & Behavior

Masha has been **spayed and microchipped**. She is an **indoor only** cat.

- **Vomiting:** She will vomit up a small amount of food once every couple of weeks (normal for her). If you see this, clean with yellow antibacterial spray from under kitchen sink and paper towels.
- **Bad behavior:** She will occasionally use her claws to ask for play. When she does this, ignore her or ideally walk away to show her that this isn't ok.
- **Weather sensitivity:** May get slightly spooked by fireworks or thunder, but generally handles it well
- **Health:** No medications or health conditions

## Daily Routine & Energy Levels
- **5:00 AM - 10:00 AM:** Most active period after breakfast - great time for play and interaction
- **10:00 AM - Evening:** Sleeps most of the day
- **Evening:** Active again, enjoys playtime
- **9:00 PM:** Goes to sleep for the night

## Favorite Activities & Toys

Masha doesn't get treats often, but feel free to give her one if you'd like!

- **Teaser wand with feather**
- **Laser pointer**
- **Spring toys** (independent play)
- **Open windows** on nice days (just remember to close before leaving!)
- **Brushing** with green brush, small white bin in wicker cabinet
